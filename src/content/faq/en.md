groups:
  - title: Applying
    items:
      - question: What homes do you have?
        answer: Four semi-detached studios with a mezzanine, one studio apartment and a four-bedroom house.
        featured: true
      - question: Who can rent?
        answer: During the academic year, our homes are for university students only, with a minimum stay of 10 months. In July and August, anyone looking for a place in Porto is welcome, with a minimum stay of one week.
        featured: true
      - question: How do I book?
        answer: Send us a request by email or WhatsApp with the home and dates you're after. If it's available, we'll call you to arrange a visit and then email you the contract. If it's full, you can ask to join the waiting list.
        featured: true
      - question: Can I visit before booking?
        answer: Of course. Get in touch and we'll arrange a visit.
  - title: Contract and payments
    items:
      - question: How much does it cost?
        answer: It depends on the home and your dates. Get in touch and we'll send you an up-to-date quote. Water, cable TV and internet are included; electricity is paid separately.
        featured: true
      - question: Which months does the contract cover?
        answer: Contracts are 10 months by default. Every faculty has its own calendar, so tell us which months you'd like to leave out; the home is vacated during those months.
      - question: Is cleaning included?
        answer: No, but you can add a cleaning service for an extra fee.
  - title: Living here
    items:
      - question: Are the homes furnished?
        answer: Yes, they're furnished and come with an equipped kitchenette. For a full academic year, bring your own bedding, towels and kitchenware. For a few weeks' stay, you don't need to worry.
        featured: true
      - question: How many people can stay in each home?
        answer: The studios and studio apartment are designed for one person. For stays of up to a month, two people at most.
      - question: Can I have visitors?
        answer: Yes, we love a good get-together. Parties need approval, and visitors can't stay longer than a weekend.
      - question: Is smoking allowed?
        answer: Not inside the homes. Outdoors only.
      - question: Can I hang things on the walls?
        answer: No. Fixing anything to the walls goes against the rental agreement.
      - question: Are pets allowed?
        answer: Unfortunately not.
